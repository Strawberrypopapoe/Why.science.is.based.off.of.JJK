Have you ever heard of redshift and blueshift? This is a phenomenon where depending on how a light source and its observer move relative to each other, the color or wavelength of the light will change.
Redshift happens when the observer and light source are moving away from each other, and blueshift occurs when the light source and observer move closer together. Now, if you take a close look at Popular anime "Jujutsu Kaisen," you'll find that there are some similarities too big to ignore.
Looking at Gojo's cursed technique, Hollow purple, is made up of two opposing forces: Attractive force, Amplification blue, and repelling force, reversal red. If you note, this is eerily similar to the redshift and blueshift theory of the Big bang that had been previously discussed.
